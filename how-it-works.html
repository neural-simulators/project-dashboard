What one benchmark attempt looks like

Each attempt is a single exchange. The model receives one prompt: the instruction, two pictures of the room, the rules the simulator will check, the required answer format, and the whole room as data, with every object's size, position, orientation, mass and friction. It answers once: new object poses for a static edit, or a launch velocity for a dynamic task, written either as a short Python program or as plain numbers.

The simulator then executes the answer exactly as given. Nothing is repaired, rounded or asked again, and deterministic checks decide whether the instruction holds. The walkthrough below follows one real attempt step by step, at your own pace: the full prompt, the exact answer format, each model’s reply, what the harness does with it, and every check with its measured value and limit.
