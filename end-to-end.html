  • GPT-5.4 built all 10 of its scenes at rest on the first try. Qwen3-VL-30B built none: Instruct sinks objects into the floor or into each other, Thinking places them in mid-air. After one repair round from PhysX's error messages, 1 (Instruct) and 2 (Thinking) of 10 build.
  • 43 of GPT-5.4's 60 tasks are valid: 3 already hold with no action, 14 were not solved by 112 PhysX tries (64 random, 48 refined), mostly throw and ramp tasks.
  • Topple tasks are all easy (55-92% of random spins or pushes succeed) because the writers asked only for the object to fall over; the next wave asks for a direction or an obstacle.

  • Given the instruction alone, GPT-5.4 agrees with PhysX on 69% of rollouts; most misses are successes it rejects over terms the instruction leaves open ("at rest" with 1-2 cm/s of residual motion; which contact came first).
  • Given the simulator's definitions and contact times, GPT-5.4 agrees on 90% (kappa 0.80) and still never calls a failure a success.
  • Qwen3-VL-30B Instruct stays at 56-72% with or without the definitions; images alone are its weakest evidence. Rows so far cover GPT-5.4's tasks; Qwen-written tasks and the Thinking reviewer follow.

Setup

  • Actions (one per task, applied at t = 0): place, drop, throw (position + velocity), push (velocity), spin (angular velocity). PhysX runs 3 s at 120 Hz.
  • Task writers: GPT-5.4 (Azure, default reasoning) and Qwen3-VL-30B-A3B; each designs scenes, then one task per category per scene. Success conditions are trees of checkable predicates (distance, contact, inside, on top of, toppled, at rest, ...).
  • Difficulty: share of 64 random actions of the task's allowed types that succeed. Easy: at least 13 of 64 (20%); hard: at most 5 of 64 (8%), including tasks solved only by refining the best random actions; medium in between. Cut-offs set once from wave 1's spread (about a third of tasks in each) and then kept fixed.
  • Future-state models: same inputs (object tokens: shape, size, mass, friction, bounciness, start pose, action) and output (each movable object's position, orientation and contacts every 0.1 s for 11 s). Trained on rollouts from scenes that are not in the task suite.
