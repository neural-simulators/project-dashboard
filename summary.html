Measures↑ contents

What the columns mean
Error
average distance of moving objects from the engine's positions over 3 s (m).
Through
deepest pair overlap beyond the engine's own, per simulation (cm).
Overlap
worst-moment overlap beyond the engine's (cm).
Bystanders
objects the engine keeps still that move more than 5 cm.
Moved early
objects that move before anything touches them.
Topples caught
share of the engine's topples the model also topples.
Verdict
the task outcome from the predicted motion equals the engine's.
