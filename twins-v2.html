Twins v2

Simulation-ready twins from casual photographs along GATOR's route, with open models, scored by GATOR's protocol and settled in Isaac Sim.

Can an open generator plus placement and a repair agent give simulation-ready twins of real rooms, the way GATOR does, without GATOR's code?

The route is GATOR's: a few casual photographs per object, masks prompted by the annotation, cameras either known (the capture's own) or estimated from the frames alone (VGGT), a multi-view generator (Pixal3D's public multi-view weights, the model GATOR's own flows were initialised from), GATOR's placement step (translation and one scale, orientation fixed), and an agent that repairs the mesh in Blender. Every number below uses GATOR's appendix-C protocol against the laser scan (ScanNet++) or the artist model (HANDAL), plus the test GATOR only shows anecdotally: does the asset stand up in the simulator?

How to read the tables↑ contents

Every measure, defined
ADD-SB
Chamfer distance between the posed prediction and the reference, divided by the reference diameter, with no alignment: shape and placement together. ADD-SB < 0.1 is the share below 0.1.
CD, NC, F1
After a similarity ICP: mean two-way nearest-neighbour distance over the reference diameter; mean absolute normal agreement; harmonic mean of precision and recall at 0.02 D.
IoU2D
Silhouette IoU of the posed mesh against the view masks, averaged over the object's views.
Reference diameter D
Convex-hull diameter of the scan instance (ScanNet++) or of the artist model (HANDAL).
Stands
After 3 s alone on its support plane in Isaac Sim: tilt of the up axis below 10 degrees and horizontal drift below 5 cm.
